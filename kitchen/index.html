Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Flooring Expert modular kitchens different from local carpenters?

Local carpenter kitchens often face misaligned hinges, bubbling edges, and moisture damage. We use industrial CNC calibrated machines for 0.1mm cutting precision, factory-sealed PUR edge-banding, certified BWP 710 marine ply, and European soft-close hinges with 10-year warranty coverage.

Do you service both Hyderabad and Delhi NCR?

Yes! We have dedicated site measurement teams, design consultants, and modular installation crews operating actively across both Hyderabad (Jubilee Hills, Gachibowli, Kondapur, Banjara Hills) and Delhi NCR (Gurugram, Noida, South Delhi, Faridabad).

How long does delivery and on-site assembly take?

Once your 3D design and material finishes are signed off, precision manufacturing takes approximately 30 to 40 days at our facility. On-site installation is completed in just 48 to 72 hours without messy dust or saw-cutting at your home.

What warranty do you provide on kitchen woodwork and hardware?

We provide a 10-year replacement warranty on BWP wooden carcass structural integrity against delamination/termites, and up to 15 years warranty on genuine Blum and Hettich drawer runners and soft-close hinges.
