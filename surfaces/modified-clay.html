Eco-Friendly Cladding.

MCM (Modified Clay Materials) offer an incredibly lightweight, breathable, and flexible cladding solution that mimics natural stone, brick, and wood effortlessly.

Explore Details

The Green Building Material

Formulated using natural soils and unfired clay, MCM is 100% environmentally sustainable. It is highly moldable, reducing transportation and installation costs while providing top-tier aesthetics.

Key Highlights:

  • Sustainable: Zero emissions; unfired manufacturing process.
  • Breathable: Prevents moisture build-up within the wall envelope.

Performance Features:

  • Frost and freeze-thaw resistant
  • Authentically mimics wood, leather, and brick

Product Specifications FAQ

What is 'Modified Clay' exactly?

It's a high-tech material made from natural recycled minerals that have been reshaped into flexible stone-like panels. This makes them environmentally friendly and highly adaptable.

Can it be installed on curved walls?

Yes. One of its key benefits is that it is flexible and moldable, making it perfect for columns, curved corridors, and complex interior geometries.

Is the surface durable enough for public spaces?

Absolutely. The panels are tough and scratch-resistant, designed to adapt to changing environments while maintaining their premium look.
