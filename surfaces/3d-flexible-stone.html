3D Flexible Stone
Dramatic Depth.

Deeply textured, three-dimensional flexible stone panels. Bring dramatic depth, shadow play, and the rugged beauty of nature to any interior wall.

Explore Details

Sculptural Stone Veneer

Transform flat surfaces into tactile, three-dimensional sculptures. Our 3D Flexible Stone is composed of a natural stone layer (slate, marble, or sandstone) bonded to a fiberglass/resin backing, featuring a pronounced relief pattern while retaining enough pliability to curve around architectural columns.

Key Highlights:

  • Deep Relief: Pronounced textures that cast stunning shadows.
  • Pliable Form: Wraps effortlessly around curves and columns.

Performance Features:

  • Modular, seamless interlocking capabilities
  • Ultra-lightweight at ~1.5 - 2.0 kg/sqm
  • Translucent for backlighting effects
  • Eco-friendly and sustainable materials
  • Low maintenance and easy to clean
