The Anatomy of Bekker Engineered Wood

Unlike solid wood that warps with moisture, Bekker Engineered Wood is constructed of multiple layers of plywood bonded together under heat and pressure, topped with a thick veneer of genuine premium hardwood. This provides the exact look and feel of solid wood, with superior dimensional stability.

Multi-Layer Construction:

  • 1
    UV-Cured Finish Layer Provides supreme protection against scratches, stains, and fading.
  • 2
    Real Hardwood Veneer (Top Layer) A 2mm - 4mm thick slice of genuine oak, walnut, or exotic timber.
  • 3
    Cross-Ply Core & Backing High-grade plywood layers stacked cross-grain to prevent warping and bowing.

Why Choose Engineered over Solid Wood:

  • Higher resistance to moisture and temperature fluctuations
  • Compatible with underfloor heating systems
  • More sustainable and eco-friendly use of premium timber
  • Easier and faster floating installation (Click or T&G)
  • Can still be sanded and refinished multiple times
