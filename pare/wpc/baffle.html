Lightweight architectural baffle ceilings featuring R50 & R35 profiles. Ideal for airports, malls, and modern offices, offering a seamless authentic wood look at just ~140 grams/r.ft.

Innovative Open Cell Architecture

PARÉ Baffle systems offer a versatile, easy-to-install solution for masking overhead plenums while keeping HVAC, lighting, and sprinklers accessible. The lightweight construction ensures minimal structural load.

Available Profiles:

  • R50 Standard robust baffle for large commercial applications.
  • R35 Sleeker, refined profile for intricate ceiling designs.

Key Baffle Advantages:

  • Ultra-Lightweight (~140g per r.ft)
  • Improves Room Acoustics
  • Conceals messy overhead services
  • Fire Retardant (D-s3;d0 European Standard)
  • 100% Water, Termite & Borer Proof
