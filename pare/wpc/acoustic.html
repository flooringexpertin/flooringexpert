Acoustic Brilliance

Specifically engineered to add depth and visual rhythm while providing sound-absorbent properties, PARÉ Acoustic panels are waterproof, moisture-resistant, and fire-retardant. With Zero VOC emissions, they are safe for indoor environments and designed for high longevity with minimal maintenance.

Key Highlights:

  • Sound Absorption: Visually pleasing louvers that actively reduce ambient noise and reverberation.
  • Durability: Highly impact-resistant with no deformation observed under rigorous testing.
